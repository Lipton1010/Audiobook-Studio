# Audiobook Studio icon concepts

Six original vector directions, September 12, 2026.

Open Audiobook-Studio-Icon-Concepts.ai in Illustrator to edit the complete comparison board. Each numbered concept is grouped. The SVG geometry was authored locally, imported into Adobe Illustrator 2026, visually checked, and saved using Illustrator’s native AI format with PDF compatibility.

Each direction includes an editable SVG icon, a 1024 pixel transparent PNG, a Windows ICO containing 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 pixel images, and a separate single-color mark SVG. White internal shapes in the single-color studies represent knockouts against white.

01 Folio: deep teal, cream and mint; open pages with sound bars.
02 Narrator: amber and ink; headphones surrounding an open book.
03 Resonant A: coral, cream and plum; an A with an audio pulse.
04 Ribbon: aubergine, pink and lavender; a flowing bookmark symbol.
05 Storybird: blue, ivory and gold; a page forming a bird.
06 Imprint: ink and gold; a book and sound enclosed in a seal.

Folio is the strongest balanced starting point. Narrator communicates audiobooks most directly. Resonant A offers the clearest initial-based identity. These are concepts for selection and refinement. Small-size previews are included for comparison; installed Windows shortcut appearance has not been tested.

The application and its existing icon have not been changed.
