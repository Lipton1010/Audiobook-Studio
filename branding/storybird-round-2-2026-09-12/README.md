# Storybird: round 2

Six variations responding to the request for a clearer bird and book/page connection.

Palette preserved from the original Storybird: blue #215BC6, warm white #F6F3E9, yellow #F8C75D.
Tagline preserved: A page takes flight and finds its voice.

01 Chapterwing: an open book forms both wings.
02 Paperfold: a folded page corner; closest to the original angular approach.
03 Pagefan: three pages fan into a rising wing.
04 Page Turn: a curling page becomes a sweeping wing.
05 Booktail: an open book forms the tail of a compact bird.
06 Storysong: a book-shaped wing, visible page lines and a golden right page.

The AI master contains grouped editable vector concepts and live Arial text. Vector geometry was authored as SVG, imported into Illustrator 2026, visually checked, and saved in native AI format with PDF compatibility. The board includes 48, 32 and 16 pixel size studies.

Each icon is supplied as SVG, 1024 pixel transparent PNG, and Windows ICO with 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 pixel images. Blue silhouette studies are included as SVG and PNG; white internal eye/curl details are white shapes.

These are design alternatives for selection and refinement. Actual installed shortcut appearance has not been tested.
